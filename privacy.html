2. The data we collect

2.1 Data you give us

  • Sign-in data. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive a unique account identifier and, depending on what you allow, your email address and name. With Sign in with Apple you can choose to hide your real email address.
  • Support messages. If you contact us, we receive your email address and whatever you write to us.

2.2 Data collected automatically

  • Game progress. Your level, in-game currency, restored cars, purchases and settings, linked to your account so you can continue on another device.
  • Device and technical data. Device model, operating system version, language, country/region (derived from IP address), app version and crash or error information.
  • Advertising identifiers. The Apple Advertising Identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking) or the Google Advertising ID, plus IP address and information about ads you see and interact with.
  • Gameplay and usage data. How you play, for example which levels you start and finish, session length and which features you use.

2.3 Purchase data

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your payment card details. We receive a transaction record (product purchased, price, date, store transaction ID and subscription status) so we can deliver what you bought.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, and the Game has no chat or public profile features.

4. Advertising and your choices

The Game is free to play and shows ads through Google AdMob. Some ads are optional (for example, watching a video for an in-game reward); others may appear between levels.

  • Consent (EEA/UK/Switzerland). When you first open the Game, a consent message lets you choose whether ads may be personalized. You can change your choice at any time from the Game's settings.
  • iOS tracking permission. On iPhone and iPad, the Game asks for permission before accessing your device's advertising identifier. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be based on tracking across other apps and websites. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Google's use of data for advertising is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

6. International transfers

Some of our providers process data outside the European Economic Area, mainly in the United States. Where this happens, the transfer is protected by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (for certified providers) or by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long we keep your data

  • Account and game progress: for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, we delete this data within 30 days.
  • Inactive accounts: if an account is not used for 24 months, we may delete it.
  • Purchase records: as long as required by Hungarian tax and accounting law.
  • Support emails: up to 2 years after the conversation ends.
  • Advertising and analytics data held by Google is kept according to Google's own retention settings.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all associated game data at any time from the Game's settings (Settings → Delete account), or by emailing us at [contact email] from the address linked to your account. Deleting your account permanently removes your progress and any unused virtual items. It does not cancel an active subscription – please cancel subscriptions in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • have your data deleted;
  • restrict or object to certain processing, including processing based on legitimate interest;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time (this does not affect processing that already happened).

To use these rights, email [contact email]. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In Hungary this is the Nemzeti Adatvédelmi és Információszabadság Hatóság (NAIH), Falk Miksa utca 9–11, 1055 Budapest, naih.hu. You can also contact the authority in the country where you live.

10. Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Players between 13 and 16 in the EEA should only agree to personalized ads with the permission of a parent or guardian; without that consent, only non-personalized ads are shown. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections, access restricted to your own account in our database, and reputable cloud providers. No system is completely secure, but if a data breach affects you, we will notify you and the authorities as required by law.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game or the law changes. We will change the “Last updated” date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the Game.
