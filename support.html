Frequently asked questions

My progress didn't carry over to my new phone

Progress syncs when you sign in. On the new device open Settings → Account and sign in with the same Apple or Google account you used before.

I bought something but didn't get it

Open Shop → Restore Purchases. If it still doesn't appear, email us with the date of the purchase. Apple sends a receipt to your Apple ID email that helps us find it.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, find the purchase and choose Request a refund.

How do I remove ads?

The Legend Cars Custom Pass in the shop removes forced ads for good and gives +20% Gears. Optional reward videos stay available if you want the bonus.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Open Settings → Privacy in the game to change your consent. On iPhone you can also go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings → Delete account in the game, or email us from the address linked to your account. All progress is permanently removed within 30 days.

I'm stuck on a level

Boosters (Wrench, Turbo and NOS) don't cost a move. Matches next to cardboard boxes crack them, and power-up blasts break them in one hit. Combining two power-ups by swapping them makes a huge combo.
