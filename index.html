How it plays

Puzzle. Wrench. Repeat.

Every level you clear pays for real work on the car on the lift. The whole loop fits in a coffee break.

  1. Swap and blast

    Drag a part one step to line up three or more. Chain cascades and hit the goals before your moves run out.

  2. Spend Gears on jobs

    Levels pay out Gears. Spend them on the next garage job and watch the car change in front of you.

  3. Sell and roll on

    Finish every job and sell the build to a collector. Then the next wreck rolls in.

Power-ups

Bigger matches. Bigger booms.

Turbo Rocket

Match 4 in a line

Clears a whole row or column with a flaming streak.

Wrench Bomb

Match in an L or T

Blows out a 5×5 area and shakes the whole board.

Rainbow Rim

Match 5 in a line

Zaps every part of one kind. Swap two power-ups together for a monster combo.

The story

Meet the crew

A family garage, a stack of unpaid bills and one rival who wants it all. Every car you restore keeps the lights on a little longer.

The bank sent another letter. If Legend Cars Custom can't pay by the end of the season, the garage gets sold, and Ryuga Kurosawa is waiting to buy it.

Under the tarp sits a rusty old rally car. Restore it, sell it, and keep the doors open. Then do it again.

Pops

Master mechanic

Hiro Tanaka owns the garage and has fixed more cars than anyone can count. His old rally partner left him one last project.

Yuna

Tuner & racer

Yuna Sato is the shop's tuner and test driver, and the only one who actually reads the manuals.

Kenta

Car dealer

Kenta Mori is young, loud and always on the phone. Restore it and he'll find a collector who wants it.
